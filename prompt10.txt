- `filterTypes(arr)`, `sanitizeQuestionTypes(arr)`, `sanitizeInstruments(v)` — helpers

- **`Setup.jsx`** — `capabilities: scope.capabilities`, `selectedArtistsCount: scope.effectiveSelectedArtists.size`, auto-reset wired via setters.
