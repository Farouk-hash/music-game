### Lyrics option shape — v40 (CRITICAL)

**`lyrics-track` options use the snippet text as `value` AND `correctValue` — NOT `opt-N`.**

The v39 `opt-N` convention was abandoned in v40 because:
- The reveal screen in SuddenDeath printed `opt-2` as raw text.
- Traditional `lyrics-track` already used snippet text, so two conventions coexisted for the same type name.

**Correct shape:**
```js
options: [{ label: snippet, value: snippet }, ...]
correctValue: own.snippet
```

Client comparison `picked === correctValue` works. `SuddenDeathReveal` renders the correct snippet naturally.
